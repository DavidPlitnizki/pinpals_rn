Pinpals
Places you've been. Moments worth keeping.

A private map of the places that meant something to you

Drop a pin where you've been, and keep it with what actually happened there — the photos, a note, how it felt, who you were with. Months later, the map remembers for you.

What it does

  • Save a place in seconds Search, or tap anywhere on the map. Add a photo, tags, and a pin colour; mark it a favourite, or somewhere you still want to visit.
  • Keep the memory, not just the dot Attach notes with photos, a mood, and the people who were there. Each memory keeps its own date, so a place you return to builds up a history.
  • Find your way there Walking, driving, or cycling directions with step-by-step instructions, and one tap to share the route with whoever's meeting you.
  • Look back A timeline of everything you've saved, filtered by period, tag, or favourite — with a memory of the day and stats on where you keep returning.

Your data

Your places, notes, and photos are stored on your device. There is no feed, no followers, and no one else browsing where you've been. Sign in with Apple or Google, or skip it entirely and use the app as a guest. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from inside the app.
